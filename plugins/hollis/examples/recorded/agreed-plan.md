# A neighbourhood repair cafe (agreed plan)

Fictional integration demo. Revised from the original plan after a real
`/plan-eng-review`. This exported copy changes only local evidence references;
the complete host transcript is retained privately.

We want a small website where residents book a free Saturday repair session.
Volunteers repair bicycles, lamps and small household objects. Our first event
has four volunteers, each available from 10:00 to 13:00. We plan 30-minute
slots (24 slots total).

## Agreed first version

- A booking form asks for a name, email, item category and preferred time.
  Email format is validated at submission.
- Slot availability is generated by one shared slot-grid function (4 volunteers
  x 6 slots), used both for the cached display and for insert-time validation
  — not two separate implementations of the same grid.
- The server checks live availability at insert time and enforces a database
  uniqueness constraint on `(volunteer_id, slot_start)`. If the slot filled
  since the page loaded, the resident sees "that slot just filled, pick
  another" rather than a silent double-booking.
- The booking is committed first; the confirmation email is sent as a
  best-effort follow-up (one retry) and never blocks or fails the booking.
- The form displays available slots from a cached schedule refreshed every
  hour; the underlying data always reflects the latest booked/completed state.
- Everyone can submit the form without an account.
- A volunteer dashboard lists bookings and can mark repairs completed, gated
  behind a shared passphrase or magic link for the 4 named volunteers — no
  resident accounts. Booking status is an explicit `booked -> completed |
  no_show` transition (no un-completing).
- Bookings retain name and email for 30 days after the event, then
  auto-anonymize to category + slot + completed/no-show. Anonymized records
  are retained indefinitely to compare attendance across future events.

## Test requirements (from eng review)

- Slot-grid generator produces exactly 24 non-overlapping slots.
- Two concurrent submissions for the same slot: exactly one succeeds.
- Dashboard access without the volunteer passphrase is rejected.
- Malformed email is rejected with a field error, not a silent failure.
- Confirmation-send failure does not roll back or block the booking.
- Retention job anonymizes bookings older than 30 days only.
- Status transition rejects `completed -> booked`.

## NOT in scope

- Multi-event / recurring scheduling.
- Resident accounts or login.
- Waitlists for full slots.
- SMS reminders, payments, admin analytics beyond attendance counts, i18n.

## Assumptions

- Single event, single day, 4 fixed volunteers — no need for volunteer
  self-scheduling or shift management in v1.
- "Minimal personal data" is satisfied by time-boxed retention (30 days) of
  identifying fields, not by never collecting them.
- Confirmation is email-only; no SMS channel assumed.
